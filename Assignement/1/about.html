Our story

BookHaven started as a single shelf in a coffee shop in 2015. A decade later, we've grown into a full store — but we've kept the same idea: books chosen by people, for people.

  • 2015One shelf, one coffee shop, and a lot of borrowed books.
  • 2018Our first real storefront opened on Willow Street.
  • 2021Started hosting monthly author readings and book clubs.
  • 2026Still small, still stubborn about only stocking books we'd actually recommend.
